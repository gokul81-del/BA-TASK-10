Business Performance Analysis – Tableau Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes business performance using Tableau Public. A Heat Map and different visualizations are used to identify sales patterns across categories, regions, and customer segments.

Dataset

File: business_performance_heatmap.csv

- Main Fields

- Category

- Region

- Customer Segment

- Sub-Category

- Sales

- Profit

- Quantity

- Orders

Tableau Sheets

Sales Heat Map – Category × Region using Sales

Sales Trend – Sales by Region and Category

Sales Distribution – Sales by Customer Segment

Sales vs Profit – Relationship between Sales and Profit

Quantity Distribution – Quantity Histogram

Interactive Dashboard

All visualizations are combined into one Tableau dashboard with an interactive Region filter.

Key Insights

Technology shows strong sales performance across the regions.

West region records high sales for several categories.

Customer segments contribute different shares of total sales.

Higher sales values generally correspond with higher profit values.

Quantity values are concentrated within a specific range.

Business Recommendations

Focus on strong-performing categories and regions while maintaining product availability.

Review lower-performing category-region combinations and develop targeted sales strategies.
